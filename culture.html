The Culture of Afghanistan

Afghanistan has a rich and diverse cultural heritage. Every region has its own traditions, clothing, food, celebrations, music, handicrafts and customs. Exploring Afghan culture helps visitors discover the history, identity and hospitality of its people.

TASTE OF AFGHANISTAN

Traditional Foods

🍚

Kabuli Pulao

Afghanistan's famous traditional rice dish

+
Kabuli Pulao is one of the best-known Afghan dishes. It is commonly prepared with rice and may include carrots, raisins, nuts and meat.
🥟

Mantu

Traditional Afghan dumplings

+
Mantu are steamed dumplings traditionally prepared with a filling and served with yogurt and sauce.
🫓

Bolani

Popular Afghan stuffed flatbread

+
Bolani is a thin stuffed flatbread commonly filled with potatoes, herbs or other vegetables.
🍜

Ashak

Traditional dumplings with herbs

+
Ashak are traditional Afghan dumplings commonly filled with herbs and served with yogurt.
AFGHAN CELEBRATIONS

Traditional Weddings

Afghan weddings are important family and social celebrations. Customs may differ from one region to another, while hospitality, family gatherings, traditional clothing, music and food remain important parts of many celebrations.

👰
