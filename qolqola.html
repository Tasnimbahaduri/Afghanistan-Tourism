Shahr-e Gholghola – The City of Screams

Shahr-e Gholghola is one of the remarkable historical treasures of Bamiyan. Rising above the valley, its ancient ruins tell a story of settlement, defense, trade, cultural exchange, and conflict. Its location along an important branch of the Silk Road connected Bamiyan with wider networks stretching across Central and South Asia.
The history of the City of Screams is also closely connected with the wider history of Bamiyan. The nearby Buddha niches and Buddhist caves demonstrate the valley's importance during the Buddhist period, while Shahr-e Gholghola represents another important phase of the region's historical development.
Today, the ruins of Shahr-e Gholghola remain a valuable part of Afghanistan's cultural heritage. Although much of the ancient city has disappeared, its remaining walls and archaeological landscape continue to provide valuable information about the past.
For travelers visiting Bamiyan, Shahr-e Gholghola offers an opportunity to combine history, archaeology, photography, and spectacular mountain scenery in one destination. Standing among the ruins and looking across the Bamiyan Valley allows visitors to experience a place where ancient civilizations, trade routes, religions, and cultures once came together.
The preservation of Shahr-e Gholghola is therefore important not only for Afghanistan but also for the wider understanding of Central Asian history. Protecting this site ensures that future generations can continue to learn about Bamiyan's remarkable past and appreciate the cultural heritage that has survived through centuries of change, he preservation of Shahr-e Gholghola is important because archaeological sites contain information that cannot easily be replaced once it is lost.
